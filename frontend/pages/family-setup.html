Questions

Where do I find my child's student ID?

It's in the welcome email we sent when your child joined (it looks like S-0012). You can also ask us on WhatsApp and we'll tell you.

Can my child still log in on their own computer?

Yes. Each child keeps their own login — their student ID (or email) and their password. Your family login is for you to see everything in one place.

I don't know my child's password

No problem — message StemNest Academy on WhatsApp. We can set a new password or create the family login for you.

Are credits shared between my children?

No, each child keeps their own credit balance. You can see every child's balance from your family login.
